Investors don’t buy an office for pretty renders. They buy numbers and trust in the developer. STRATA puts them together into a decision in one scroll: from a tower above the park to the financial model.

Task

Where we started

A 34-storey office tower by a lake in Kyiv, before sales open. Buyers are investors and companies looking for a headquarters.

The website had to give them everything for a first decision and lead to a single action: requesting the investment memorandum.

  • 01Show trust arguments on the first screen, not at the end
  • 02Explain a four-block tower so it’s easy to explore
  • 03Let investors calculate their own return

Idea

Layers

Strata means “layers”. The mark is a circle cut into floors. Shift the layers and the circle becomes a four-block tower with a garden on every setback. The same idea drives the whole site: you read it layer by layer, from the big picture to the numbers.

Process

Cut in five stages

Every project goes the same way: from a raw idea to a website that shines and works.

Stage 01

Raw material

Audience: Class A investors and tenants. What they need to know before the first call and what they’re afraid of.

Stage 02

Marking

The order of arguments: trust, tower, floors, autonomy, yield, location, developer, FAQ, request.

Stage 03

Cutting

A mark made of layers, a dark graphite palette, pink accent only on the main action, a large grotesque.

Stage 04

Setting

An interactive tower with block hotspots, an area slider with a yield calculation, a route map, an animated mark.

Stage 05

Polish

Two languages, a mobile version with a sticky request button, speed and its own subdomain.

Solutions

What we did and why

01

Trust on the first screen

The developer’s four guarantees sit right under the headline, before any renders. Investors first see who they’re giving their money to.

02

A mark that becomes a tower

Strata means “layers”. A circle cut into floors assembles into a four-block tower as you scroll. The brand idea explains the building.

03

The tower as navigation

Instead of a floor menu, the building itself. Hover a point to see the block, click to jump to its layouts.

04

Autonomy as an argument

Power, heat, water and shelter get their own section. For a company it means uninterrupted work, and it’s shown in numbers.

05

Investors do the maths

The area slider instantly shows the price, rent, fit-out cost and yield. People come to the call with their own number.

06

Location with routes

Metro, city centre, airport: hover a place to see the route and travel time. “Easy to get to” becomes specific.

Mobile version: the request button always under your thumb.

Result

What the developer gets

A landing page that works like a first meeting with the sales team: it explains the property, answers questions about risk and lays out the financial logic. The person who calls has already decided the project interests them.

Concept project: the office tower and developer are fictional, figures are illustrative. The website is fully working.
