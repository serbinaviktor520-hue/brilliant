Privacy policy

This website belongs to Victor Brilliant (Brilliant), designer and art director. This page explains what data you leave through the website and what happens to it.

What data is collected

Only what you enter yourself in the forms and chat: your name, phone number, Telegram or WhatsApp handle, a link to your website, industry, budget, a text or voice message.

Why

To get in touch with you, answer your request, prepare a proposal or a website review. The data is never used for newsletters without your consent and is never sold to third parties.

Analytics

The website uses Google Analytics to count visits and requests in aggregate. It uses cookies and does not identify you personally.

Where it is stored

Requests arrive in my personal Telegram. The website is hosted on Vercel, which may keep technical request logs.

How long it is kept

As long as needed to work together or answer your request. You can ask me to delete your data at any time.

Contact

For anything about your data, message me on Telegram @vitya_brilliant or email hello@vbrilliant.com.
